Executive Overview & Problem Statement

Understanding the daily commute challenges on the Malad–Jogeshwari BEST bus corridor.

Background

The Malad–Jogeshwari transit corridor serves as a critical artery for thousands of students and daily commuters in Mumbai. With several educational institutions and commercial hubs along this route, the BEST bus network is the primary mode of affordable public transport.

The Challenge

During peak hours, this corridor experiences severe congestion, leading to a cascade of operational and commuter challenges:

  • Peak-Hour Student Rush: Overwhelming demand outstripping supply during college start and end times.
  • Traffic Bottlenecks: Unpredictable delays caused by narrow road segments and illegal parking.
  • Waiting Time Uncertainties: Significant discrepancies between scheduled times and actual arrivals.
  • Boarding Hazards: Overcrowded stops leading to unsafe boarding practices and stampede-like situations.

Actionable Recommendations & Policy Insights

Proposed solutions to alleviate commuter stress and optimize depot operations.

Short-Term

Special Student Shuttles

Deploy point-to-point shuttle services linking major railway stations directly to educational clusters during key college timings (7 AM - 9 AM & 12 PM - 2 PM).

Short-Term

Organized Physical Queues

Install barricades and employ traffic wardens at high-density stops like Ismail Yusuf College to ensure orderly boarding and prevent stampedes.

Long-Term

Dynamic GPS Dispatching

Transition from static schedule-based dispatching to demand-responsive dispatching utilizing real-time GPS fleet tracking and crowd density metrics.

Long-Term

Digital Pass Integration

Mandate and optimize RFID-based digital student passes via the Chalo app to reduce cash transaction times during boarding, speeding up the dwell time at stops.
